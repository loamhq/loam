# Archive trial report

## Purpose and scope

The Archive trial began on 2026-10-06. Its purpose was to test whether the new catalogue could preserve packet names and retrieval order when an archive was moved between rooms. The trial included 48 packets from Shelf B. It excluded photographs and audio recordings, which will remain in their existing catalogue until a separate trial has been completed. The original packet labels were retained throughout the trial; the trial did not introduce a new naming scheme.

## Method and results

The team entered the packets in their original shelf order and then asked a second reader to retrieve them using only the catalogue. Each retrieval was checked against the original label. Of the 48 packets, 46 were retrieved on the first attempt and 2 required label corrections. The corrected labels were B-17 and B-32. No packet was lost, and no contents were changed. A repeat check on 2026-10-13 retrieved all 48 packets in their original order. The repeat check used the corrected labels, with the original names retained beside them in the catalogue. The team kept a written record of each correction so that the trial could be repeated without relying on memory.

## Cost

The trial cost $1,248.75: $900 for catalogue entry and $348.75 for the independent retrieval checks. The budget was $1,500. The unused $251.25 remains in the archive budget. No funds were moved to another project, and the trial did not commit the archive to any continuing subscription or service. These figures cover the completed trial only and do not estimate the cost of a wider rollout.

## Decisions and next review

The review retained the original packet order and accepted both label corrections. It postponed expansion to Shelf C until the next review on 2026-11-03. At that review, the archive coordinator will decide whether to include photographs in the next trial. Audio recordings remain excluded. No expansion was approved in this report. The report closes with these recorded decisions and makes no recommendation about which material should be trialled next.
