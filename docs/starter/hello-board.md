## Things to try

Six things that make Loam yours. They are in no order and you can skip any of them. Delete this list whenever you like; Iris can bring it back.

- [ ] **Make an agent.** Someone with a character of their own, for one part of your life.
- [ ] **Change Loam by asking.** How it looks or what it does.
- [ ] **Build a small app.** A tool or a page of your own, live at your own address.
- [ ] **Look around the Market.** Themes, plugins and skills other people have made.
- [ ] **See what happened overnight.** Your agents wake by themselves. Ask Iris what she did.
- [ ] **Open your vault.** Everything here is a plain file that belongs to you. Start with `About me`.
