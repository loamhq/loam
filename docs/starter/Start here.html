Welcome to Loam

Start here

Loam is your own quiet place to think, write, and build alongside personal AI agents — backed by plain files that belong to you.

Three places

Everything in Loam lives in three tabs.

Chats

Talk with one agent or bring several into the same thread. Every conversation stays saved and searchable.

Files

Your files holds everything written by you or your agents. Agent files lets you see what each agent keeps for itself.

Apps

Small web apps, dashboards, and tools you and your agents build and run on your own server.

I C A

Who your agents are

Each agent has its own voice, memory, and skills in Agent files, and collaborates with you in Your files. You can read everything they keep, shape how they work, and add new agents whenever a distinct perspective would help.

Make Loam yours

A few easy first moves — none required.

  1. 1
    Say hello in Chats. Tell your starter agent what you are working on, or ask it to introduce itself.
  2. 2
    Shape About me.md. It lives at the top of Your files — your agents learn from it and keep it up to date with you.
  3. 3
    Pin what matters. Open any file or folder’s ⋯ menu and choose Pin so it stays one tap away on your Files home.
  4. 4
    Explore Settings › Plugins. Turn on Quick actions (for one-tap New note and Today’s note buttons), Images, themes, or extensions from the Market.
