# Loam 0.2.12

The Market, said plainly, and Dictation and Read aloud that work on their own.

## The Market

Loam has a Market, in **Settings › Market**: themes and plugins you add to
your Loam with one tap, each made and kept by its maker in a repository of
their own.

- **Your agents can find things there too.** Ask one for "a darker theme" or
  "something to read my notes aloud" and it searches the Market and puts an
  install card in the thread, in any thread. Tap the card to see the listing
  and install it.
- **What is in it today:** five themes (Autumn, Deep Forest, Foundry, Mist
  Trail and Yosemite), Syncthing for syncing your vault to your devices,
  Dictation and Read aloud. Each is its maker's, listed in their name.
- **What comes with Loam:** two themes (Latte and Plain), Daily notes,
  Comments & suggestions, Git backup and Images. Images used to be in the
  Market; it is built in now, off until you turn it on in Settings › Plugins.

## Changed

- **Dictation and Read aloud carry their own server.** Turning speech into
  text and text into speech used to be done by Loam itself, so every Loam
  carried that code whether or not you used either plugin. Now each plugin
  brings its own small server and Loam has none of it: a Loam without them
  is lighter, and they work on any Loam that can run plugins. You won't see
  a difference: your microphone, your terms note, your preferences on each
  device, your Google key and each agent's voice are as they were.
- **Read aloud's listing says one thing about its key.** It said both "Uses
  your Google Gemini key" and "Asks for a key of its own". It uses your
  Google key from Settings › Connections › API keys, and that is all the
  listing says now. No listing can show both for the same key.
- **Each agent's reading voice is a Read aloud setting.** The voices your
  agents read in (and the character of each) were part of Loam's own setup.
  They are Read aloud's now, under its Advanced settings, carried over on
  the update. A voice you chose on an agent's profile still comes first.

## Updating

Update from Settings › About Loam, or type `loam update` in your server's
shell. Nothing to do by hand. If you have Dictation or Read aloud, Loam
updates each to its newest version from the Market at its first start
after the update, keeping its settings and keys; a copy you edited
yourself is left as it is, and needs updating from the Market before it
works on 0.2.12. Audio you had already made for Read aloud is made again
the first time you play it.
