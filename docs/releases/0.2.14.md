# Loam 0.2.14

See where your plugins came from and keep them up to date.

## Plugins

- **See the source.** Settings › Plugins shows whether a plugin came with
  Loam, from the Market, from a link, or from your own files. Open its
  details to go to its listing or folder.
- **Updates happen automatically.** Loam checks Market listings and linked
  repositories daily. Automatic updates are on by default for those plugins;
  you can turn them off in About. Activity shows updates as they happen.
- **You decide about new access.** If an update asks for more access, Loam
  waits for you to review it in About.
- **Your edits stay yours.** Editing an installed plugin pauses its
  automatic updates. You can also make a separate copy to customise; the
  copy takes precedence and the original remains available.

## Also changed

- Market listings show Loam's review beside the listing's actions when a
  review is available.
- File viewers and the editor now match the rest of the app's surface.
- New conversations use a connected AI provider for their automatic
  defaults. The welcome conversation offers a few ways to get started.
- Conversation handling is more resilient to interruptions and keeps
  messages in order while an agent is working.

## Updating

Update from Settings › About Loam, or type `loam update` in your server's
shell.
