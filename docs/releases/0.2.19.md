# Loam 0.2.19

## For your agent

- **Garden.** To add Garden, paste its repository link into Install from a
  link in Settings › Plugins. Review its access and confirm. Open Garden in
  Apps to see the island grow with your work. During the first two weeks,
  growth or an agent’s idea can bring a Garden update into Chats.
- **Hello and the first week.** Hello starts with an empty board. An
  untouched starter checklist on an older install is cleared on update.
  Boards you have edited stay yours. Iris can mark your first week with
  a message during days seven through thirteen, including after a missed
  reflection.
- **People who customize plugins.** Updates protect local changes. A
  matching published version can be recognized in place. When the files
  differ you can review upstream changes with your agent or skip a version.
  Market pages can show the downloads GitHub has counted.
- **People using speech.** Dictation and Read aloud can use Google, OpenAI
  or browser speech. The browser option explains how to connect a shared
  provider key in Settings when you want to use a hosted provider.
- **Chats and Files.** A returning plugin line can restore its counts before
  the list appears. Branches show the quoted message. Files can move an
  eligible item to trash and offer Undo.
- **Reporting a problem.** Your agent can prepare a scrubbed bug report,
  show you the exact preview and follow the issue after you approve sending.
