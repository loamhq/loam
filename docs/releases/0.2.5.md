# Loam 0.2.5

The Market's first listings, and plainer settings for Images.

## New

- **The Market has Loam's own plugins in it.** Images, Syncthing and five
  themes (Autumn, Deep Forest, Foundry, Mist Trail and Yosemite) are listed
  in Settings › Market. Get one there and Loam shows what it reaches before
  you say yes, as with any plugin.
- **Syncthing**, for anyone: keep your vault on your own phone and laptop,
  synced with your server directly. Give an agent a device's ID and it pairs
  the device and shares your vault with it; ask it how syncing is going and
  it tells you.

## Changed

- **Two themes are built in: Latte and Plain.** The other five moved to the
  Market. If you were using one of them, or had changed its settings, the
  update installs it for you from the Market (or from its own copy, if
  GitHub can't be reached), and it stays chosen. A theme you never used
  isn't installed; get it from the Market whenever you like.
- **Images' settings read like settings.** Where images are saved, and which
  service makes them ("Whichever key you have", OpenAI or Google Gemini),
  each with a line saying what it means. The model names are under
  Advanced. Update Images from the Market to get them.
- A theme from the Market brings its own display font, shown as soon as it
  is chosen.

## Updating

Update from Settings › Update, or type `loam update` in your server's
shell. Nothing to do by hand.
