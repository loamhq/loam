# Loam 0.2.20

## For your agent

- **New installs.** Open Garden in Apps to see an island grow with your
  agents, chats, projects and files. Garden 0.1.1 comes installed and
  enabled from its reviewed Market release. The Hello board starts empty.
  Garden’s Chats line appears during the first two weeks when something
  grows or an agent has an idea.
- **Existing installs.** You can find Garden in the Market and choose
  whether to install it. Settings › Plugins lets you turn Garden off or
  remove it.
- **Chats.** Chats shows its rows as soon as they are ready. On a return
  visit, it remembers the space used by a plugin’s line while that line loads.
