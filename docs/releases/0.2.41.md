# Loam 0.2.41

## For your agent

- **Small · Everyone.** New threads now pick up a short, plain title automatically after your second message, suggested by the replying agent from what the conversation is about. A title you set or edit yourself is never overwritten.
- **Small · People who rename an agent (or renamed one earlier).** Renaming an agent from its profile page (**Settings › Agents**) or through an idle rename now moves its folder, id, wake, schedules, and inbox to match its new name while keeping its conversations, history, and settings intact (renaming waits if the agent is mid-reply). On update, any agent that was renamed earlier has its folder and wake moved automatically to match its name, with its conversations and history kept and nothing asked of the owner.
- **Small · People who browse or install plugins from the Market.** When Loam's review notes differences between what a plugin's code does and how its listing describes it, the Market now shows **Differences Loam noted** directly on the install and update screens before you say yes (and **Differences from its description** in Loam's review note). Listings without differences look unchanged.
- **Behind the scenes.** Market review standards and proof cases distinguish imprecise descriptions (passed with Loam's note) from concealed behavior (refused), temporary-directory cleanup removes read-only scratch directories cleanly, and private test systemd managers clean up reliably on teardown.
