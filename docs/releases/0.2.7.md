# Loam 0.2.7

Telling Loam's makers, without a GitHub account.

## New

- **Send Loam's makers a bug, a wish or an idea privately.** Ask your agent
  to "tell the people who make Loam" anything: something broken, something
  you want, or just a thought ("I wish…", "this confused me", "I love…").
  It writes it up with your name, your agents' names, addresses and keys
  taken out, shows you every word, and on your yes sends it to a private
  inbox only Loam's makers read. No GitHub account is needed. The makers
  read every one; they may not answer.
- **Ideas are a kind of their own**, beside bugs and feature requests.
- **The GitHub route stays.** If you would rather follow your report in
  public, your agent can still give you a link that opens it as an issue on
  GitHub, filled in, for you to submit from your own account.

## Updating

Update from Settings › About Loam, or type `loam update` in your server's
shell. Nothing to do by hand. An install that has not updated can still
send a report through GitHub.
