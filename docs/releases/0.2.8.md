# Loam 0.2.8

Loam on a domain of your own.

## New

- **Install Loam on your own domain.** The installer now asks where your
  Loam will live: on a domain you have (for example `loam.example.com`, or
  `example.com` itself), or on a name Loam's makers give you, as before.
  Say it in the command with `--domain loam.example.com` or `--name sam`,
  or answer when asked.
- **It tells you exactly which DNS records to make, and checks them.** Two
  records, both type A, pointing at your server: your domain and
  `*.` in front of it, so your agents' own sites work too. The installer
  says in plain words which one is still missing or points somewhere else,
  spots an old IPv6 record or Cloudflare's proxy getting in the way, and
  waits while you make them. When they are right, Loam goes on the web by
  itself, with a certificate for each address from Let's Encrypt. Nothing
  else to set up: no keys for your DNS company. `loam status` says the same
  until it is done.

## Fixed

- **The installer can ask its questions.** Run without its options, the
  installer stopped at the first question as if it had no screen to ask
  on; it now asks your name, your time zone and where Loam lives, as it
  always meant to.

## Updating

Nothing changes for a Loam that is already installed. Update from Settings ›
About Loam, or type `loam update` in your server's shell.
