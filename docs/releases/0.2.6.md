# Loam 0.2.6

Settings in seven, and kinder empty pages.

## Changed

- **Settings has seven places instead of nine**, in this order: Account,
  Appearance, Agents, Connections, Plugins, Files and About Loam. On a phone,
  Activity is still the first row.
  - **Account** is who you're signed in as, your password, your passkeys and
    Sign out. It was Sign-in.
  - **Connections** is one page in three groups: AI providers (was
    Providers), Services such as GitHub (was Integrations), and API keys.
    The API keys look and work as before; only where they live changed.
  - **About Loam** is your version and Update, and the server Loam runs on:
    its processors, memory, storage and how long it has been running. It was
    Update.
  - Links and agents that open an old place still land in the right one.
- **A plugin's page is plainer.** Under its name: who made it, its version,
  and View source. The repository and commit are behind View source.
- **A new agent's draft card says who they are in a plain line**, such as
  "Sam's kitchen companion", instead of the first words of their
  instructions. The agent drafting them can now write that line itself.
- **Empty pages look like Loam.** A place with nothing in it yet (no chats,
  an empty Market, a search with no match, an empty folder) shows one quiet
  drawing and one line, in your theme's colours.

## Updating

Update from Settings › About Loam, or type `loam update` in your server's
shell. Nothing to do by hand.
