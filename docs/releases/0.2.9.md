# Loam 0.2.9

Images is built in, and everything in the Market is its maker's own.

## New

- **Images is part of Loam.** Making and changing pictures with OpenAI or
  Google is a built-in plugin now, like Daily notes and Comments &
  suggestions: it comes with every release and is updated with Loam. On a
  new install it starts off; turn it on in Settings › Plugins. It uses the
  same OpenAI or Google key from Settings › Connections › API keys, and your
  agents' `generate_image` tool works as before.
- **If you installed Images from the Market, nothing changes for you.** On
  the update, the Market copy steps aside for the built-in one: it stays on,
  and its folder, model choices and keys are kept as they were. A copy you
  edited yourself is left as it is.
- **Every Market listing lives in its maker's own repository**, one
  repository per plugin, under the maker's name. The five themes and
  Syncthing moved to theirs; Autumn leads the Market.

## How plugins are made now

Two kinds, as in Obsidian. Built-in plugins ship inside Loam and are turned
on in Settings › Plugins. Market plugins are made and kept by their makers,
in their own repositories. Something new starts in the Market in its maker's
name, and one that proves itself can later become part of Loam.

## Updating

Update from Settings › About Loam, or type `loam update` in your server's
shell. Nothing to do by hand. A theme you got from the Market keeps working
as it is.
