# Loam 0.2.9

Dictation and Read aloud move to the Market.

## Changed

- **Dictation and Read aloud are Market plugins now**, each in its maker's
  own repository, rather than part of Loam. A new Loam has neither: find them
  in Settings › Market (or Find more plugins in Settings › Plugins) and press
  Install. Until one is installed and on, there is no microphone in the
  composer and no Listen or Brief under a reply.
- **If you had either on, nothing changes for you.** When your Loam updates,
  each one you had on is installed from the Market for you, still on, with
  your preferences on each device, each agent's voice and your Google key
  kept as they were. If the Market cannot be reached at that moment, the copy
  that came with this release is put in its place, and the Market can update
  it later.

## Updating

Update from Settings › About Loam, or type `loam update` in your server's
shell. Nothing to do by hand. Reload the page once after the update to see
the plugins in Settings › Plugins as installed from the Market.
